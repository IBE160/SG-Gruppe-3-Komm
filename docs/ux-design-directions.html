Design Direction Showcase

Design Philosophy: Clarity and Focus

The recommended design direction is minimalist, component-driven, and focused on clarity. This approach aligns with the application's purpose: to provide a calm, organized, and efficient tool for students. By prioritizing content and functionality over ornamentation, we create a user experience that reduces cognitive load and helps users focus on their primary goal of planning their studies.

The aesthetic is clean, modern, and spacious, built upon the solid foundation of Shadcn UI and Tailwind CSS. A consistent typographic scale, a clear visual hierarchy, and generous use of white space guide the user's attention naturally through the interface.
