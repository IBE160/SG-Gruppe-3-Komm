## Action Planning

### Top 3 Priority Ideas

#### #1 Priority: What are the biggest technical risks we need to mitigate?

- Rationale: Addressing technical risks upfront is critical. It prevents costly rework later, ensures the project stays on schedule, and builds a more reliable and secure application, which is essential for gaining user trust.
- Next steps: 1. Risk Brainstorming Session: Schedule a 1-2 hour meeting with the technical team to brainstorm potential risks. 2. Create a Risk Register: Document each risk, its potential impact, its probability, and a mitigation strategy. 3. Prioritize Risks: Rank the risks to focus on the most critical ones first.
- Resources needed: Time from the lead developer and/or architect. A project manager to facilitate and document. A shared document or a tool (like Jira or a simple spreadsheet) for the risk register.
- Timeline: The initial risk register could be created within the first week of the project. It should then be reviewed at the beginning of each sprint.
